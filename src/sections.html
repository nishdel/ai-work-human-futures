How this is calculated · career method 1.0.0

Published evidence: O*NET describes U.S. occupations. The ILO studies GenAI exposure. Neither supplies or validates this app's scores.

Author/model assumptions: task shares, mappings, domain curves and all scoring weights. These are editable planning assumptions, not statistical estimates. Physical work, safety and regulation already act inside domain curves; no extra bonuses are added.

Visitor answers: your confirmed tasks and anchored preparation choices. Missing answers are not zeros.

Task exposure = sum of normalized task shares × each mapped domain's deterministic curve at the selected year. Technical capability and actual substitution are calculated separately. It is not unemployment probability.

Preparation = weighted average of answered components. All four required answers are needed for a score. Optional unanswered components are omitted and the remaining weights are renormalized. Resource constraints are not personal failings.

Resilience = 75% × preparation + 25% × (100 − modeled actual task substitution). Scores are rounded to whole points; they are not statistical confidence estimates or measures of human worth or hiring suitability.

Worked example: with all required answers at 50 and optional answers omitted, preparation is 50. If modeled actual substitution is 60%, resilience is round(0.75 × 50 + 0.25 × 40) = 48. A fully prepared person can still have high exposure.

Source coverage and assumption sensitivity are shown separately from scores. Try different task mixes and scenarios; there is no invented confidence interval.

Profile answers stay in page memory by default. No accounts, analytics, storage or AI API. The app does not transmit your answers; the hosting provider may receive ordinary request metadata. Opening a source link contacts that website. Clearing your profile cannot erase copies you previously exported.
